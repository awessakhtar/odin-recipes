Blueberry Pancakes


Description


Thick, diner-style pancakes bursting with sweet, juicy blueberries.


Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 1 cup fresh blueberries

Process

  1. In a large bowl, sift together flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt.
  2. Make a well in the center and pour in milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Mix until smooth, taking care not to overmix the batter.
  4. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat.
  5. Pour batter onto the griddle, using roughly 1/4 cup per pancake.
  6. Drop a handful of blueberries onto the top of each pancake.
  7. Flip when bubbles form on top and the edges look dry.
  8. Cook until brown on both sides and serve with syrup.
