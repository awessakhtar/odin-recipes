Garlic Parmesan Pasta


Description


A quick, rich, and comforting pasta dish perfect for busy weeknights.


Ingredients

  • 8 oz fettuccine pasta
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 3/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Process

  1. Cook fettuccine in boiling salted water according to package instructions.
  2. Heat olive oil and butter in a large skillet over medium heat.
  3. Add minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Pour in chicken broth and heavy cream, bringing to a simmer.
  5. Let the sauce simmer for 5 minutes to thicken slightly.
  6. Stir in the Parmesan cheese until completely melted and smooth.
  7. Toss cooked pasta into the sauce until fully coated.
  8. Season with salt and pepper before serving hot.
