Tomato Basil Bruschetta


Description


A vibrant Italian appetizer featuring toasted bread topped with seasoned fresh tomatoes.


Ingredients

  • 1 French baguette, sliced into rounds
  • 4 large Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic glaze
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Process

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet.
  2. Place baguette slices on the sheet and brush lightly with olive oil.
  3. Toast bread for 6 to 8 minutes until crisp and golden.
  4. In a bowl, combine diced tomatoes, basil, garlic, and olive oil.
  5. Season tomato mixture with salt and pepper to taste.
  6. Let the mixture sit for 10 minutes to marry flavors.
  7. Spoon the tomato mixture generously over the toasted bread.
  8. Drizzle with balsamic glaze immediately before serving.
