Chicken Stir-Fry


Description


Crisp vegetables and tender chicken tossed in a savory, glossy soy sauce glaze.


Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breast, cut into bite-sized cubes
  • 2 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 carrot, thinly sliced
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil

Process

  1. Whisk soy sauce, honey, and sesame oil in a small bowl.
  2. Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat.
  3. Add chicken cubes and cook until no longer pink inside.
  4. Remove chicken from skillet and set aside on a plate.
  5. Add broccoli, bell pepper, and carrot to the hot skillet.
  6. Stir-fry vegetables for 4 minutes until tender-crisp.
  7. Return chicken to the skillet and pour the sauce over everything.
  8. Toss continuously for 2 minutes until the sauce coats everything.
