Chocolate Chip Cookies


Description


Soft, chewy cookies packed with rich chocolate chips and a hint of vanilla.


Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 3/4 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 1 egg + 1 egg yolk
  • 2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips

Process

  1. Preheat your oven to 325°F (165°C).
  2. Sift flour and baking soda together in a medium bowl.
  3. Cream melted butter, brown sugar, and white sugar together.
  4. Beat in vanilla, egg, and egg yolk until light and creamy.
  5. Mix in the dry ingredients until just blended.
  6. Fold in the chocolate chips by hand using a wooden spoon.
  7. Drop cookie dough onto baking sheets using a spoon.
  8. Bake for 15 minutes or until edges are golden brown.
